# Problem Statement

You are given multiple lines of text as input. Each line may contain the word "thy" appearing one or more times.

Your task is to process each line and replace **all occurrences** of the word "thy" with the word "your".

The replacement must be:
- Case-insensitive (e.g., `thy`, `Thy`, `THY`, etc.)
- Applied only to the **whole word "thy"**
- Not applied to substrings (e.g., "theory" should remain unchanged)

---


# Sample Input

```id="7q9a2j"
Feed'st thy light's flame with self-substantial fuel,
Thy self thy foe, to thy sweet self too cruel:
thy Thy tHy THY theory
```

---

# Sample Output

```id="9rfy7p"
Feed'st your light's flame with self-substantial fuel,
your self your foe, to your sweet self too cruel:
your your your your theory
```

---

# Solution

```bash id="yytg4n"
sed 's/\<thy\>/your/gi'
```

---

# Explanation

* `s` → Substitute command in sed
* `\<thy\>` → Matches the **whole word "thy"** only
* `your` → Replacement string
* `g` → Replaces **all occurrences** in each line
* `i` → Makes the match **case-insensitive**
